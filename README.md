# NextGen1z (N1z)

**Different Teams. One Identity.**

NextGen1z is a multi-team organization built around collaboration, creativity, and a shared vision. We bring different teams together under one identity, making space for people to contribute their perspectives, work together, and build toward common goals.

## What brings us together

- **Collaboration** — teams and people working together, not in isolation.
- **Creativity** — room to explore ideas and approach challenges from different perspectives.
- **Shared vision** — a common identity that connects our work across teams.

Different teams. Different strengths. One community moving forward together.
